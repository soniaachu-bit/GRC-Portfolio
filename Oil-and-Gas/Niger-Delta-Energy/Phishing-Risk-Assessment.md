# Phishing Risk Assessment for Niger Delta Energy Ltd
Employees in Niger Delta Energy Ltd recently identified multiple suspicious emails impersonating the Finance Director. The emails requested recipients to review an urgent financial document through an external link. One employee clicked the link but did not enter credentials. Although no credentials were compromised, the incident highlighted potential weaknesses in the company's ability to detect and prevent phishing attacks.

This assessment was conducted to evaluate the organization's exposure to phishing threats, identify vulnerabilities, assess associated risks, and recommend controls to reduce the likelihood and impact of future incidents.

# Company Background
- Company: Niger Delta Energy Ltd. (Hypothetical)
- Industry: Oil & Gas (Upstream)
- Employees: 500
- Locations: Headquarters in Port Harcourt and 3 field offices
- Business Operations: Oil exploration, Drilling operations, Logistics and transportation, & Vendor management.
  
# Objective: 
The objective of this assessment is to identify phishing-related risks affecting Niger Delta Energy and recommend controls to reduce the likelihood and impact of successful phishing attacks.

# Critical Asset
The following assets were identified as potentially vulnerable to phishing attacks:
- Employee email accounts
- Financial systems
- Vendor payment information
- Corporate network
- Sensitive operational data
