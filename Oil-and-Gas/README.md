# Oil and Gas Cybersecurity GRC Projects

This directory contains practical cybersecurity Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) case studies focused on the oil and gas industry.

The projects explore cybersecurity risks, information security management, security controls, and compliance practices within hypothetical oil and gas organizations.

## Industry Focus

- Sector: Oil and Gas
- Operational Areas: Upstream, Midstream, and Downstream
- Geographical Context: Nigeria / Niger Delta
- Focus Areas: Risk Management, Information Security, Compliance, and Security Governance

## Projects

1. Phishing Risk Assessment

Status: Completed

Assessment of phishing-related cybersecurity risks within a hypothetical Nigerian upstream oil and gas company.

The project examines phishing threats, organizational exposure, potential business impacts, and recommended security controls.

2. ISO 27001 Gap Assessment

Status: In Progress

Assessment of an organization's existing information security practices against ISO/IEC 27001 requirements to identify gaps and recommend improvements.

3. Third-Party / Vendor Risk Assessment

Status: Planned

Evaluation of cybersecurity risks associated with external vendors and service providers supporting oil and gas operations.

4. Cybersecurity Risk Register

Status: Planned

Identification, analysis, prioritization, and documentation of cybersecurity risks affecting a hypothetical oil and gas organization.

## Frameworks and Standards

- ISO/IEC 27001
- NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)
- Risk Management Principles
- Information Security Governance

## Project Documentation

Each project will include relevant business scenarios, assessment objectives, identified risks, analysis, security controls, findings, and recommendations.

## Disclaimer

All organizations and business scenarios used in these projects are fictional and created for educational and portfolio demonstration purposes.

These projects do not represent actual assessments conducted for any real organization.
